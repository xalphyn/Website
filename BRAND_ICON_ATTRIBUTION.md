# Social icon sources

The bundled Instagram, YouTube, Threads, Facebook, X, Reddit, Bluesky, Discord, Medium, Spotify, SoundCloud and GitHub logo paths originate from **Font Awesome Free Brands**. The icons are subject to the Font Awesome Free icon license (CC BY 4.0); https://fontawesome.com/license/free . Brand marks and names remain trademarks of their respective owners, with no claimed endorsements.

Audiomack and Substack use clearly labelled monochrome **initials** (`AM` and `S`) rather than inaccurate lookalike logos. Official Audiomack assets are available from https://styleguide.audiomack.com/ . Replace the initials with verified brand assets if exact logos are required.
