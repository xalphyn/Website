The beginning of Programming Hub.

A Discord space for developer friends that opened to a wider community in 2026.

Programming Hub is a Discord community founded by XALPHYN for people interested in programming, sharing projects, asking questions, and meeting fellow developers.

28 July 2026 — The private beginning

According to XALPHYN's account, the server was created on 28 July 2026 as a private space for programming friends. It started as a way to bring conversations and shared interests together.

4 August 2026 — Public launch

Programming Hub was officially announced and opened more broadly on 4 August 2026. Its intended direction is a welcoming space for learning, collaboration, resources, and conversation among programmers at different levels.

A community under maintenance

At present, XALPHYN describes the server as inactive while its setup is being completed. There is no confirmed reopening schedule. Its public Discord invite remains listed for anyone who wants to find the project.

A separate purpose

Programming Hub is related to XALPHYN as its founder, but it is distinct from the music-centered SynthBeatsWaves initiative. The two projects have their own communities and identities.

A place to learn, build and connect — one conversation at a time.

Public references

Public directory entries also describe Programming Hub as a project by XALPHYN. These are external community-directory descriptions and may not reflect current membership or server activity.
