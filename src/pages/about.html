About / The artist’s story

Behind the name XALPHYN.

An emerging independent music artist and founder of SynthBeatsWaves, with a second community project bringing developers together.

XALPHYN is an emerging music artist and public digital identity. “My existence is my silent era.” is the signature bio behind the name. The identity's main focus is music: discovering sounds, publishing tracks, and growing a community around them.

Where it began

On 18 July 2025, XALPHYN posted the first AI-assisted Synthwave track, “Neon Dreams,” marking the beginning of SynthBeatsWaves (SBW). What started with one upload developed into a broader music community with tracks across styles and a growing presence on social platforms.

SynthBeatsWaves — music for creators

SynthBeatsWaves is a music community of artists focused on providing non-copyright tracks. It exists for listeners, editors, video creators, and other artists who want music to discover and incorporate into their own creative projects. Music videos, releases, and community activity—not a chat server—are the heart of SBW.

Early tracks were created using AI-powered music tools, while XALPHYN has also been learning to produce original music. A dedicated SBW website is planned to bring together artist information, published tracks, future releases, articles, and downloadable music with clear per-track usage terms. Read the full SBW article for more detail.

Rise of Tron

Before a prolonged pause in posting, XALPHYN published “Rise of Tron,” a Synthwave track associated with anticipation for Tron: Ares. XALPHYN reports that it became the first breakout track, passing 40,000 views and continuing to draw listeners even during months without new uploads. This milestone is founder-reported, not a live independently verified count.

Programming Hub — connecting developers

Beyond music, XALPHYN initiated Programming Hub as a separate community project where developers can connect, exchange ideas, discuss code, and learn from one another. It began privately among programming friends on 28 July 2026 and was announced publicly on 4 August 2026.

For now, Programming Hub is a Discord community server whose setup remains under maintenance. It serves a different audience and purpose from SBW.

A quieter chapter

Examinations, studies, and other personal commitments have reduced the time XALPHYN can spend on music and community work. Music uploads have been inconsistent, and both community-server setups still need attention. The hope is to return to publishing, continue refining music-production skills, and move the projects forward when time allows, without promising a particular return date.
