SynthBeatsWaves.

SynthBeatsWaves (SBW) is a music community of artists founded by XALPHYN to provide non-copyright music tracks for listeners, video creators, editors, and other artists. Its main work is publishing music and developing a welcoming creative community across social platforms.

The separate SBW website is coming soon, with plans for artist profiles, track releases, articles, and music downloads accompanied by clear usage permissions.

Programming Hub.

Programming Hub is a separate community project created by XALPHYN to connect developers, programming enthusiasts, and friends through a Discord server. Members can discuss programming, exchange ideas, and learn from one another.

The server began as a private group and was later opened publicly. Its setup is still being finalized.
