Privacy / Information

Privacy & external links.

How this portfolio handles visitor enquiries and what happens when you leave for outside platforms.

Website visits

This is a static portfolio website. The hosting provider may process server requests, IP addresses and technical device information for delivery, diagnostics and security. The site itself does not include advertising trackers or third-party analytics libraries in its supplied files.

Enquiry submission form

The contact form asks for your reply email, a subject, your message and an optional name. If the website is hosted with Netlify and form detection is enabled, Netlify Forms processes and stores those submissions for the site owner to review privately. The owner may separately enable email notifications in the private Netlify dashboard. Do not submit sensitive credentials or confidential information.

To request that an enquiry be reviewed or deleted, send another message through the contact form and describe the request; identity verification may be necessary. This is a basic project privacy notice, not a promise of a specific legal retention period.

Anonymous messages through NGL

The anonymous message option leads to NGL, a separate external service. Messages are sent there, not through this website's form. Not having to type a name is not a guarantee of complete technical anonymity.

External accounts and community links

Social links, community invitations, YouTube videos and Medium links lead to third-party platforms with their own privacy rules and account systems.

Cookies, analytics and future changes

No third-party analytics tracker, ad tracker or embedded social widget is bundled into this site. The deployment provider can still use technical mechanisms for hosting and protection. If analytics or other integrations are added in the future, this page should be revised before public launch.
