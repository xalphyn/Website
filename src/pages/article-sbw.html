SynthBeatsWaves: non-copyright music for creators.

A growing music community of artists founded by XALPHYN, sharing tracks intended for creators and listeners across different genres.

SynthBeatsWaves (SBW) is a non-copyright music-providing community of artists founded by XALPHYN. Its purpose is to publish and share music for listeners, video editors, content creators, and artists who want accessible tracks for creative projects. SBW is a music-first community with its own social presence, published music videos, and plans for a dedicated website.

How the music began

The project started with XALPHYN's AI-assisted Synthwave track “Neon Dreams.” From there, additional music videos explored different genres and sounds. Early releases used AI-powered tools, while learning to make independently produced compositions remains part of XALPHYN's musical development.

Sharing tracks with creators

The vision of SBW is straightforward: give people a place to discover music suitable for content creation and connect with a community of artists around that music. Public videos share the tracks; a future download catalog is planned to organize releases and make usage conditions easy to understand.

Important permission detail: Calling a track “non-copyright” describes SBW's intended creator-friendly approach, not a blanket legal guarantee. Each track should clearly state its actual license, whether commercial use or attribution is permitted, and any limits arising from AI-generation services or third-party material. The upcoming SBW website can publish those conditions alongside each downloadable release.

Rise of Tron and the community's momentum

The Synthwave track “Rise of Tron” became a significant milestone. According to XALPHYN, it crossed 40,000 views and kept attracting listeners during a period without new uploads. The count is founder-reported, rather than a verified live metric.

What SBW is building

SBW aims to keep publishing music across styles, introduce participating artists as the community grows, and provide a dedicated website for articles, upcoming tracks, releases, and downloads. Its direction is music distribution and artist collaboration, rather than a single discussion server.

What's next?

The pace of publishing has been affected by studies and other commitments. As time allows, XALPHYN plans to continue music production, release more tracks, and expand the SBW community. The dedicated website has not launched yet, so its release catalog and permissions should not be treated as already available.
